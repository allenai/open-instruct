    Long-context prepared datasets

    MILES consumes prepared document reasoning, retrieval and transduction tasks using versioned source adapters and reward contracts. Dataset selection, inventory, hygiene, pool assignment, screening and release assembly belong to RL Data Workbench. Open Instruct provides the adapters, verifiers and training loader that Workbench calls.

    Ownership

    Component Owner
    Candidate revisions, explicit train/held-out split roles, row dispositions and context variants Workbench
    Decontamination, duplicate policy, quality filters, pool taxonomy and immutable releases Workbench
    Qualification orchestration, screen/trial protocols, analysis and evidence Workbench
    Source messages and verifier targets, reward implementations and runtime loading Open Instruct
    Mixture weights, sampler, prompt/response budgets and training MILES run configuration

    The adapters lc_docqa_v1 and lc_golong_v1 preserve source messages and native subtasks. Adapter identity is explicit metadata, including through routed_v1. Adapters do not assign a pool. Workbench's labeling policy decides that; GoLong math can share a pool with document tasks while retaining its math verifier.

    Consume a prepared release

    Render a declared model/context variant in Workbench, attach its versioned pool labels, and assemble the release. Workbench's export-open-instruct and bind-open-instruct produce prepared JSONL, a namespaced verifier registry and a run-local data.toml. Use that binding with the MILES run configuration. Preserve origin_id, record_id, release_id, labeling_version and the exported pool_id fields. Configure sampling weights in the run; the dataset does not prescribe them.

    Choose prompt and response budgets explicitly and ensure their sum fits the validated model/runtime context. A smaller prompt budget defines a different population. Silently truncating a long-context task can destroy answerability.

    Workbench owns the rl_data_workbench.readiness commands that inventory sources, qualify exported bundles through the actual loader/rewards, and exercise bounded GPU coverage. These commands require the selected Open Instruct runtime. Loader and reward checks establish compatibility, not the correctness of every example or a learning benefit. Historical screens, trials and storage locations live in Workbench alongside their pinned population and scorer identities.

    Reward contracts

    LongContextVerifier is local and deterministic. It reads the final region after the last explicit </think> when present. Preserve core.reward_final_answer_only and core.reward_zero_truncated settings across comparisons. A valid truncated response receives zero under the truncation policy; a verifier failure is unknown rather than an incorrect answer.

    Route Contract and limitations
    DocQA numeric Whole numeric answer with absolute tolerance 1e-6 or relative tolerance 1e-4.
    DocQA short answer Case/whitespace-normalized whole-answer equality, with the published answer prefix and optional trailing period. Valid paraphrases can be false negatives.
    Choice A single extracted choice; contradictory explicit answer declarations receive zero. See the parsing limitations below.
    GoLong LongBench QA Case/whitespace-normalized whole-answer equality with answer-marker extraction; contradictory extra lines are rejected.
    GoLong EM Publisher's strict, case-sensitive whole-answer equality after thinking removal; no answer-marker extraction or punctuation normalization.
    GoLong math Existing isolated math verifier.
    GoLong document sets Set precision/recall F1; blank lines ignored, extra guesses reduce reward.
    GoLong ranked retrieval Linear-relevance NDCG at the reference length; blank lines ignored, repeated guesses keep their first occurrence, and duplicate references are rejected.

    Choice extraction is a format heuristic, not a general ambiguity detector. After The correct answer is (C), it extracts C while permitting trailing option text and explanations. It does not infer whether later references to other options are proposed answers or explanatory comparisons.

    Choice parsing normalizes Markdown bold markers, single-letter LaTeX boxes (\boxed{C} or \boxed{(C)}), math delimiters around those letters and full-width parentheses. Lowercase letters require explicit parentheses or a box: (c), **（c）** and \boxed{c} are accepted, but bare c and **c** are not, even as the entire answer. Bold alone only marks capital letters as choices. This avoids extracting articles or abbreviations from **a** reduction or i.e. (B). Chinese punctuation is accepted. A closed parenthesized letter after the explicit answer prefix can directly precede option text, as in (C)仅对…. These rules apply only to choice extraction; they do not relax GoLong exact-match or numeric contracts. Conflicting explicit answer declarations are checked after normalization, so formatting cannot hide a second declared answer.

    Continuation after The correct answer is (C) Behavior and limitation
    and (D), or (D), , (D), or D., or [D], or and (d) Still extracts C; a hedged answer can receive credit.
    A newline followed by or (D) Still extracts C; a hedge can also span lines.
    The correct answer is (D) on the same or a later line Receives zero because the explicit answer declarations conflict.
    答案应是 (C), Vitamin (D) deficiency, or (see paragraph (B)) Retains C; repeated confirmation and explanatory references do not invalidate the extracted answer.

    Standalone answers without the explicit prefix use stricter whole-answer parsing after formatting normalization: **C** and $\boxed{C}$ are accepted, while (C) or (D) is rejected. This format dependence is a known limitation. Credit does not certify that the response asserts only one answer, and unsupported formatting can still cause false negatives.

    GoLong recall-only, partial-order, summary-proxy and table routes remain deferred. Unknown task identifiers fail admission rather than selecting a scorer by guess. These are versioned local contracts, not a claim of exact upstream scorer parity. Fractional rewards are retained; full-credit pass@k and mixed numeric reward probability are distinct screening measures.

    The lc_transduction_v1 adapter also supports NVIDIA Transduction. Its positional line-accuracy contract uses independently recomputed references. The pinned source is 90fa9816, under the publisher's declared OpenMDW 1.1 license.
