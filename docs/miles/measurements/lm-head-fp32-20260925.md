# FP32 LM-head: unchanged-weight comparison

Keeping the final vocabulary projection's output in FP32 modestly reduces
SGLang/Core log-probability mismatch on both hero SFT checkpoints. On identical
full prefixes, changing both heads lowers mean absolute error by **11.8% for
non-EMO** and **10.4% for EMO**. Most mismatch remains, and tail errors do not
improve consistently. Measured serving throughput is essentially unchanged.
This is an inference diagnostic, not a training-cost or learning qualification.
No training defaults were changed.

The subsequent [full-FP32 reference baseline](full-fp32-20260925.md) measures
both complete forward paths with matched BF16 controls. Its eager execution and
shorter cached workload are distinct from this head-only study.

## What changed

The LM-head projects the final normalized hidden state onto vocabulary logits:
`z = W h`, followed by `log p_i = z_i - logsumexp(z)`. Its output rounding acts
directly on the token scores and normalization used by RL probability ratios.
For example, BF16 spacing in the interval [32, 64) is 0.25. Nearby results on
opposite sides of a rounding boundary can therefore acquire a much larger
difference. This is an illustrative scale, not a measured logit distribution.
Casting an already rounded BF16 result to FP32 cannot restore those bits.
A common offset cancels in softmax; token-dependent rounding does not.

The pinned SGLang `enable_fp32_lm_head` CUDA path keeps BF16 weights and inputs,
and uses `torch.mm(..., out_dtype=torch.float32)`. It preserves the FP32 GEMM
output instead of rounding it to BF16 first. It does not make the entire model
FP32 or correct hidden-state differences introduced by attention or experts.

The Core probe captures the exact same post-normalization inputs to
`model.lm_head.w_out` and compares the default projection with this FP32-output
projection. A third control explicitly converts both operands to FP32, with
TF32 disabled. No optimizer runs; the head's weight hash is identical before
and after scoring. The final normalization is unchanged.

## Measured comparison

Errors below are absolute natural-log-probability differences between serving
and Core at unchanged weights. Each rollout arm contains 16,384 response tokens.
Each fixed-prefix arm scores the same four baseline continuations, 2,048 tokens.
The generated trajectories can differ between serving precision modes; use the
fixed-prefix comparison to control for that difference. Fixed-prefix scoring
uses full-prefix prefill, whereas rollout scores include cached decoding.

| Checkpoint | Serving head | Core head | Rollout mean | Rollout p99 | Outside 20% | Fixed-prefix mean |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Non-EMO | BF16 | BF16 | 0.035146 | 0.266732 | 2.246% | 0.035254 |
| Non-EMO | BF16 | FP32 | 0.033618 | 0.252988 | 2.069% | 0.033931 |
| Non-EMO | FP32 | BF16 | 0.033367 | 0.254273 | 2.087% | 0.033063 |
| Non-EMO | FP32 | FP32 | 0.030635 | 0.244296 | 1.770% | 0.031084 |
| EMO | BF16 | BF16 | 0.041780 | 0.330037 | 3.613% | 0.045591 |
| EMO | BF16 | FP32 | 0.040147 | 0.314126 | 3.265% | 0.043656 |
| EMO | FP32 | BF16 | 0.040278 | 0.328657 | 3.632% | 0.043504 |
| EMO | FP32 | FP32 | 0.038111 | 0.320441 | 3.223% | 0.040860 |

Outside 20% means `p_Core / p_serving` outside [0.8, 1.2], not an observed PPO
clipping rate or the upper-two TIS clipping rate. Non-EMO rollout probability
Pearson correlation rises from 0.998960 to 0.999159; EMO from 0.998312 to
0.998578. A high correlation alone does not establish negligible ratio tails.

On fixed prefixes, non-EMO p99 increases from 0.268414 to 0.273575, while EMO
p99 decreases from 0.315414 to 0.292192. Maximum fixed-prefix errors increase
from 0.705674 to 0.761093 and from 0.826831 to 0.836632 respectively. The fraction
above the upper-two TIS threshold is unchanged in these small fixed-prefix
samples: 1/2048 non-EMO and 2/2048 EMO. FP32 is not a complete numerical fix.

The efficient FP32-output Core projection closely matches the strict FP32-input
control: across both checkpoints and serving arms, maximum logit difference is
0.00009537 and maximum selected-token log-probability difference is 0.00003624.
The aggregate mismatch changes negligibly between these two controls.

| Checkpoint | BF16-head tokens/s | FP32-head tokens/s |
|---|---:|---:|
| Non-EMO | 987.44 | 992.00 |
| EMO | 989.84 | 990.88 |

These are short warmed measurements, excluding engine initialization, graph
capture, warm-up and Core scoring. Differences below 0.5% do not establish a
speedup. They also do not measure training backward cost or peak memory.
FP32 outputs can cost additional memory in a training implementation; SGLang
already converts ordinary logits to FP32 downstream. The tested flag does not
double stored head weights or require a strict FP32-input matrix multiply.
