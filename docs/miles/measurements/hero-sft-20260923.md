## Planning issue context

The September 22–23 updates compare a 100B-token architecture sweep (3:1 versus
7:1 KDA/full-attention ratios, shared versus split Q/K gains, and EMO ancestry),
then 2T/4T pretraining milestones and full Dolci Think SFT. At 4T, both MoEs
substantially improve on the roughly active-parameter-matched dense 810M baseline;
non-EMO generally leads general/base scores and 4K–64K long-context evaluations,
while EMO is stronger at the extrapolated 128K point. Those extrapolation results
do not change the exported 64K maximum context.

The SFT comparison is mixed: the selected non-EMO arm leads MATH and Alpaca at
T=0.8, EMO leads HumanEval, and IFBench ties. Moving from 2T to 4T generally helps.
Lower-temperature decoding frequently reaches the output cap, especially on
IFBench, so results are sensitive to decoding settings. Each MoE used one SFT
learning rate rather than a tuned sweep; this is not a definitive EMO ablation.
