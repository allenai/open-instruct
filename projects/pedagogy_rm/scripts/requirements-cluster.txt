# WeightTransferUpdateRequest, IPC/NCCL TrainerSendWeightsArgs and engines,
# WeightTransferConfig, FlatLogprobs, FlexibleArgumentParser, MambaSpec).

# The nvidia-* CUDA libraries are deliberately NOT pinned. Pinning them from a
# `pip freeze` of a working env pulls cu11 and cu12 wheels side by side, and they
# collide on the shared nvidia/<lib>/lib/ paths - the older cu11 file wins and torch
# dies on a missing symbol. Let torch declare its own.
