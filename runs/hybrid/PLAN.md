## 1. Working definition

- One new arm, **H = MOPD 60 → RL 240**: 300 student updates in total.
  - Updates 0–59 are MOPD, identical to the original MOPD 100 recipe.
  - Updates 60–299 are mixed RL, identical to the mixed RL 300 recipe.
- Teachers, base, data (v4 `mix_all3`), batch (64 prompts × 8), LR (1e-6 constant) and eval contract are all unchanged.
- **Transition point:** 60 = 20% of 300, taken from Kevin's "like 20%". It was not chosen from held-out results, and no other transition point will be run.
  - Disclosure: in-training 1-answer MOPD curves (which level off around update 50) were seen before this design.
- **Seeds:** 42 / 43 / 44. Seed k of H takes its MOPD stage from MOPD 100 seed k and is paired with mixed RL 300 seed k and MOPD 300 seed k.

## 5. Proposed preregistration (needs Kevin's sign-off; not yet fixed)

- **Question:** at 300 student updates on the same prompts, is MOPD 60 → RL 240 ahead of, behind, or unclear relative to pure mixed RL 300 on internal math, IF and code? Secondary: vs MOPD 300.
- **Analysis:**
  - Unchanged seedstats3: two-level bootstrap, 95%, 4,000 resamples, 256 × 8 internal evals of the final checkpoint.
  - Rule: ahead if the lower bound > 0, behind if the upper bound < 0, unclear otherwise.
  - No equivalence test; unclear is not "no effect".
- **Margin:** none exists that was approved for this question. The Oct 22 rule stays descriptive only. **Kevin to decide** whether a practical margin is wanted (§8, D4).
- **Prediction (proposed; Kevin to confirm or replace, D3):**
  - **P-H1:** H is ahead of RL 300 on IF. Basis: MOPD's per-update IF gain (+.068 [.039, .098] vs RL 100) arrives early, and RL kept improving through 300.
  - **F-H1:** IF upper bound < 0 (behind) falsifies "warm start helps IF at a fixed student budget". Unclear means not supported.
  - No directional prediction for math or code; they are guards, reported under the same rule.
- **Gates:**
  - **H0 (before launch):** hf-59 and cursor 59 present; target roots absent; config diff vs the RL 300 parent only in the fields listed in §6; validate passes.
  - **H1 (startup):** first trained rollout id 60 (D2-a) or 0 (D2-b); weights loaded from hf-59; LR 1e-6; first-step KL and reward finite.
  - **H2 (end):** 300 student updates, hf-299 (D2-a) or hf-239 (D2-b) exported, audit passes.
  - **H3:** 256 × 8 per task, 0 failed samples.
- **No exclusions;** only mechanical relaunches.

## 7. What this cannot show

- One teacher set; three seeds.
- A single transition point: no claim about the best fraction.
- Fresh Adam at the switch is part of the treatment.
- Even if H beats RL 300, that is not a compute-efficiency result, since it includes 300 sunk teacher updates.
